Delete your Nanti data

Nanti (Google Play: dev.edycu.nanti) has no accounts, so a deletion request is one email. This page explains how to ask, what is deleted, and what is kept.

How to request deletion

  1. Email edy.cu@live.com with the subject “Delete my Nanti data”.
  2. If you bought anything in Nanti, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt email, or at play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). This is how we find your record.
  3. If you never bought anything, also include the “App user id” shown in Nanti → Settings (under RevenueCat receipt) — as much of it as the screen shows. Without an id or an order number the record is not linked to you and we cannot find it.
  4. We delete the data listed below within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your RevenueCat customer record: the anonymous app user id, your purchase history as RevenueCat stores it, and the settled-ad balance (ADS) with its ad-impression events.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play order records (what was bought, when, and the amount) are kept by Google under Google’s own retention rules, which we do not control.
  • Where tax or accounting rules require it, a minimal purchase record (product, date, amount — no personal details) may be retained for the legally required period, then deleted.
  • Deletion does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data on your phone

The letters you write are never uploaded. The local ledger (letters made, ads owed/settled, last city, language) lives only on your phone. Uninstalling Nanti, or Android Settings → Apps → Nanti → Storage → Clear storage, deletes it immediately.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ad data collected when you watch a rewarded ad (advertising ID, device identifiers, ad interactions, IP address) is held by Google, not by us. Control or delete it through Google: Android Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID) and myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. See how Google uses data from partner apps.
